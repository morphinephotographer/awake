**awake.** keeps your Mac awake — right from the MacBook notch.

### Download
- **awake-1.7.2.dmg** — open and drag awake. to Applications.
- **awake-1.7.2.zip** — the same app in a zip.

The app is not notarized yet: on first launch open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (or right-click the app → Open).

### New in 1.7.2
- No more doubled icons: when the ChatGPT app and Codex CLI (or Claude and Claude Code) work at once, the notch shows one ChatGPT / Claude icon

### New in 1.7.1
- ⋯ → Apps → «App icon in the notch»: turn it off to keep the collapsed notch clean while an app renders — the icons stay in the open panel

### New in 1.7
- **AI agents:** Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Aider, OpenCode, Cursor Agent, Copilot CLI, Grok CLI in Terminal, and the Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor apps — the Mac stays awake while the agent is on a task, with 3 minutes of grace while it thinks on the server

### New in 1.6.2
- A soft green glow under and inside the open panel (static, only while the panel is open)

### New in 1.6.1
- The launch glow now shows inside the notch shape too: during the animation the shape drops a little below the notch, and a soft green light rises inside it

### New in 1.6
- English, Ukrainian and Russian — follows the macOS language, or pick it in ⋯ → Language
- Fixed: a watched app's icon could flash in the notch before the launch animation

### What's inside
- Hover the notch → ⏻ and 15m · 1h · 2h · 4h · ∞, or until a set hour
- Render watcher for ~60 photo, video, 3D, audio and download apps
- First-launch card with the apps found on your Mac, suggestions for unknown busy apps
- Closed-lid mode, keep-display, battery guard, ⌥⌘A, ⌘Q, awake:// links

macOS 14+. Created by Morphine.
